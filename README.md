#### Diwali Sales Analysis — Python EDA Project



* ###### Project Overview



This project explores Diwali sales data using Python to understand customer purchasing patterns, product category performance, and potential high-value purchase behavior. The goal is to turn raw sales data into meaningful business insights.



* ###### Tools \& Technologies



1\.Python



2\.Pandas and NumPy



3\.Matplotlib



4\.Seaborn



5.Jupyter Notebook



* ###### Dataset



The dataset contains customer demographics, location, occupation, product categories, order information, and purchase amounts.



* ###### Data Cleaning and Preparation



1\.Inspected the dataset structure and missing values.



2\.Removed columns that contained no useful data.



3\.Handled missing values in the purchase amount.



4\.Identified and removed duplicate records.



5\.Prepared the cleaned data for exploratory analysis.



* ###### Business Questions explored 



1\.What is the customer distribution by gender?



2\.Which age groups contribute most to purchases?



3\.How do gender and marital status relate to purchasing behavior?



4\.Which states and zones contribute the most sales?



5\.Which occupations show the highest purchase contribution?



6\.Which product categories generate the highest sales and average purchase amount?



7\.How are total sales, average purchase amount, and order volume distributed across product categories?



8\.What characterizes high-value purchase records, and which customers contribute the most among those records?



* ###### Key Findings



1\.The 26–35 age group contributes the highest total purchase amount.



2\.Female purchase records outnumber male purchase records in the dataset.



3\.Food generates the highest category sales, at approximately ₹3.39 crore.



4.Clothing \& Apparel has the highest order count in the category analysis.



5.Auto has the highest average purchase amount among product categories.



* ###### Business Recommendations



1.Investigate the purchasing preferences of the 26–35 age group to identify opportunities for targeted campaigns.



2\.Study the algorithm of strong Food category sales and explore ways to improve revenue in lower-performing categories.



3\.Use customer purchase patterns to develop relevant promotional strategies.



4\.Examine high-value purchase behavior to identify opportunities for customer engagement and retention.



* ###### Project Structure



1\.Python EDA Analysis Project.ipynb — analysis, visualizations, and business insights.



2\.Diwali Sales Data.csv — source dataset.



3\.README.md — project documentation.



* ###### Conclusion



**This project demonstrates the use of Python for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and business insight generation. It highlights how customer and product-level analysis can support data-informed business decisions.**
